FUTURE OF WORK HACKATHON · TRACK 1

AccessTwin

Walk a workplace before it is built, as the people it might shut out.

01

The problem

Benches, controls and signs built for one standard body quietly shut out wheelchair users, people with low vision and neurodivergent workers, and it is usually discovered after the space is built.

02

Our solution

A phone scan becomes a measured digital twin. AI agents, each living with a different disability, walk it, cite every problem and fix the design before anything is bought.

03

Live example

This hackathon room, scanned tonight, redesigned as a full robotics lab.

04

The RGB-D scan

iPhone LiDAR and video: 1,092 frames fused into 2 million colored points, aligned to the RoomPlan walls. 208 m², 3.65 m ceiling.

05

Remove people and tables

Everything that is not wall, floor or ceiling is removed geometrically, and the carpet underneath is re-tiled from a clean patch of the real carpet. No one captured in the scan survives.

06

Add everything

08

The disability agents

Four agents, each with a body model and a cited rulebook.

Wheelchair user

Manual or power wheelchair; seated eye height ~1.2 m; needs 36" routes and 60" turning space.

  1. 36" clear route from the entrance to every station, e-stop and exitADA 403.5.1
  2. 60" turning circle at the accessible stationADA 304.3
  3. Doors: 32" clear opening, opening force 5 lbf or lessADA 404.2.3, 404.2.9
  4. Controls and e-stops reachable seated: 15-48" (less over obstructions)ADA 308
  5. Work surfaces 28-34" high with knee clearanceADA 902.3, 306

14 problems found in Design A · 5 blocking

Low vision / color-blind

Reduced acuity and contrast sensitivity, or red-green color blindness (~8% of men).

  1. Sign contrast 4.5:1 or better between letters and backgroundWCAG 1.4.3, ADA 703.5.1
  2. Letter height large enough for the mounting height and viewing distanceADA 703.5.5
  3. Zone colors stay distinct for red-green color blindness (protanopia, deuteranopia)WCAG 1.4.1
  4. Zones identified by name or pictogram, not color aloneWCAG 1.4.1
  5. Room signs have raised characters and braille at 48-60"ADA 216.2, 703.2-703.4

29 problems found in Design A · 0 blocking

Neurodivergent worker

Autistic / ADHD / sensory-processing differences: sensitive to noise and motion, relies on predictable layouts.

  1. Quiet stations (desks, ESD benches, soldering, dining) at 55 dBA or less from lab equipmentANSI/ASA S12.2, PAS 6463
  2. No continuous robot motion within 2 m of a seat without a screenPAS 6463
  3. Every station reachable from the entrance in 4 turns or fewerPAS 6463
  4. One consistent sign system: same colors, layout and letter sizePAS 6463
  5. An enclosed quiet retreat roomPAS 6463

20 problems found in Design A · 0 blocking

Motion clearance

Anyone passing robot cells: the arm's full sweep must stay inside its marked or fenced zone.

  1. Each arm's full reach stays inside its taped zoneISO 10218-2:2025
  2. Fenced cells: 120 mm between the arm's reach and a 20 mm mesh fenceISO 13857
  3. 500 mm crush gap between the arm's reach and walls or furnitureISO 13854
  4. E-stops can be pressed from outside the arm's reachISO 10218-2:2025
